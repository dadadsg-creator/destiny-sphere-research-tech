# Воранеры · KADERIUM_FORGE · одноклеточный концепт V03

## Причина переработки

`V02` компактнее первого концепта, однако печь, подача руды, кран и боковые модули по игровому масштабу образуют двухклеточный промышленный комплекс. `V02` сохраняется без изменений как кандидат на такое здание. `V03` создаётся специально для одной клетки.

## Запрос

Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: Destiny Sphere 2 production concept for a single-cell Voraner Kaderium forge.

Input images: Image 1 is the current forge whose materials, function, asymmetry and adult realistic style must be preserved, but its ground footprint is still too wide; Image 2 is the accepted single-cell stasis collector and is the strict reference for maximum ground footprint and camera scale.

Primary request: redesign the forge as a genuinely compact one-cell building. Stack the industrial functions vertically instead of spreading them sideways. Keep one clearly visible orange-white furnace mouth at the front-left, a very short integrated ore feed directly beneath/next to it, a dense central machinery and pipe core, one tall rear heat-exhaust tower, and a small arched service entrance at the front-right.

Composition/framing: identical isometric gameplay-compatible view around 45°/30°. The entire physical base, rubble, stairs, pipes and machinery must fit inside a single compact roughly circular/hexagonal footprint no wider than the stasis collector's base in Image 2. The silhouette should be distinctly taller than it is wide. No external crane arm, no long conveyor, no detached yard, no side wings, no modules protruding beyond the compact foundation.

Scene/backdrop: the artificial ring-world sandy-stone surface used in the references, with enough empty ground around the building to prove the footprint.

Style/medium: highly detailed realistic adult science-fiction concept art, not cute, not simplified, not toy-like.

Lighting/mood: Silus light from screen southeast at 33–35 degrees; highlights on right-facing surfaces, attached cast shadow to upper-left.

Color palette/materials: weathered pale stone and ceramic, thin gold ribs, cobalt glass and narrow cyan energy channels; localized orange furnace glow only around the furnace.

Constraints: preserve Voraner visual language and forge function; one building only; no people, vehicles, text, labels, watermark, gardens, fountains, roads or neighboring buildings.

Replace only the large arched front-right doorway and its ceremonial stairs with an unmistakably industrial finished-product dispatch point. It must read as an automated output terminal where finished material exits the forge, never as an office, church entrance, residential door, or public staircase.
