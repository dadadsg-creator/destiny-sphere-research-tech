## Prompt

Reverse the apparent concavity. From the Silus-facing camera, the rigid ancient backing structure and central/home hives are farthest; complete neutral desert hives climb shallow faceted upper and lower slopes toward the camera; two tunnel-river conduits sit on the near lips; a transparent protective dome is the nearest enclosing shell.

Keep shared central hives in a horizontal chain. Surround each complete central hive with six homes. Put full neutral desert modules beyond the homes. Use articulated seams between complexes to absorb the small radius difference without stretching the hexagons.

The two tunnel rivers run horizontally around the ring in opposite directions and occupy no gameplay cells. The transparent dome spans between them. Preserve equal convex hexagons and full-edge adjacency. Avoid a convex bulge, cropped neutrals, continuous gameplay rows at visibly different radii, fingers, spokes, bridges, UI and text.
