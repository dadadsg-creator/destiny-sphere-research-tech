## Prompt

Look radially from the position of Silus almost exactly perpendicular to the inhabited inner cylindrical surface. Use a long-lens near-orthographic frontal view with the ring circumference running left to right.

Place three shared central hives in a horizontal chain through the middle, with the middle complex complete and neighbouring complexes continuing beyond the frame. Around every central hive are six full home hives. Several neutral desert hives form broken sandy belts toward the upper and lower margins. Beyond them are non-playable ancient structural shoulders and a transparent protective dome.

Near the upper part of the dome, show one restrained enclosed blue-white transport conduit running parallel to the ring circumference, with only a few discreet descent nodes. This provisional tunnel river carries cargo and armies and occupies no gameplay cells.

Every hive remains an equal-scale convex hexagon with six straight sides. One side touches at most one neighbour. Avoid an oblique camera, annular rows of different circumference, stretched cells, fingers, spokes, corner junctions, UI and text.
