## Prompt

Redesign the ring segment as an inward-facing semi-concave habitat band following the old DestinySphere idea of a shallow trough turned toward the central star. The playable honeycomb lies on the inner cylindrical wall at one constant orbital radius; raised side shoulders create the visible concavity but contain no gameplay cells.

Show two complete neighbouring six-player complexes and partial continuation. Each complex contains one central hexagonal hive divided into six sectors and six full home hexagons around it. Several complete neutral desert hives form a broken marginal belt near a raised shoulder.

Use a wide near-orthographic oblique view compatible with a `45° / 30°` game camera while showing the trough cross-section and the ring curving away. Silus is visible in the open sky on the concave side and lights the surface from front/lower-right at approximately `35°` elevation.

Every playable hive is an equal-scale convex hexagon with six straight sides. A side touches at most one neighbour. Avoid a flat annular disk, concentric gameplay rows of different circumference, stretched outer cells, compressed inner cells, extra fingers, spokes, bridges, Y-shaped corner junctions, gaps, UI, labels and text.
