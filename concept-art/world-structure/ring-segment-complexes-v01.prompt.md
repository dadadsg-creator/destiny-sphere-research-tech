## Prompt

Create a wide orthographic-isometric view of a short curved fragment of an immense artificial ring world assembled from exact hexagonal hives and repeated six-player complexes. Reconstruct a believable ancient-Constructor megastructure rather than a generic floating island.

Show two complete neighbouring complexes and partial continuation of a third. Each complete complex has exactly one shared central hexagonal hive divided into six wedge sectors, surrounded edge-to-edge by exactly six full home hexagonal hives. Along the outward radial perimeter place several full neutral desert hives, one per corresponding home where visible, forming a broken desert belt outside the complexes. Adjacent complexes interlock through normal full hex edges.

Use an orthographic isometric camera, 45-degree azimuth and about 30-degree elevation, wide 2:1 landscape. The ring curvature must be visible, but individual hexagons remain readable. Inner radius of ring at top, outer radius at bottom. Silus light comes from lower-right/front at 35 degrees elevation; coherent shadows extend toward upper-left. Use restrained blue-white stasis-energy accents in seams and subtle transparent protective domes over a few hives.

Every hive is one clean convex hexagon with exactly six straight sides. A full side can touch at most one neighbouring hive. Hives touch directly along whole sides. A vertex is only the meeting point of at most three hives. Preserve exact geometric logic from the topology reference. Neutral hives are multiple desert hexagons along the outer edge.

Avoid extra fingers, radial arms, spokes, roads leaving corners, Y-shaped junctions, branching bridges, gaps between adjacent hives, multiple neighbours attached to one side, non-hexagonal cells, circular platforms, floating islands, tall perimeter walls, cities, buildings, text, labels, UI and watermarks.
