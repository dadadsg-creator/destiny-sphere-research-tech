# Home hive quasi-real V01

## Назначение

Цельный художественный мастер-кандидат домашней соты до нарезки на runtime-тайлы. V01 проверяет направление: зрелая квазиреалистичная поверхность, строгая конструктивная геометрия, мелкий масштаб рельефа и свободные основания под здания.

## Промпт

Redraw the V09 target as a mature, highly detailed, quasi-realistic science-fiction landscape suitable for the final Destiny Sphere game client. Preserve the exact camera, crop, projection, central six-sided hive boundary, all six straight sides, every corner junction, surrounding neighboring-hive fragments, and the central Constructor plate. Preserve the broad terrain distribution: arid sandy upper zone, engineered soil around the perimeter, restrained green central belt, separate unconnected blue lakes in the center and lower half. Keep the landscape one continuous authored surface without visible gameplay cells.

The world is an artificial ring terrain made by the vanished Constructors. Relief must be shallow and all surface details much smaller than a one-cell building. Use serious adult science-fiction, quasi-photorealistic materials, crisp fine microdetail, believable sand, compacted mineral soil, short vegetation, shallow clear water and subtle embedded technical traces. Keep the playable area mostly unobstructed.

Silus light comes from the lower-right/front at about 33 degrees elevation, with restrained warm highlights and soft terrain shading toward the upper-left. Use slightly less saturated natural colors. No buildings, roads, bridges, connected rivers, gameplay grid, text, interface, watermark, painterly blur, miniature look, mountains, cliffs, large ruins, large rocks or tall trees.

## Статус

Художественный кандидат. Не подключать к runtime до оценки и до создания настоящего крупного мастера. Нарезка будет выполняться только после утверждения цельной композиции.
